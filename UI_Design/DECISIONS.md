- **D-023** `2026-10-01` **One experience for everyone** (Alex): no Student or Chef version. Role is only a label in the profile (badge, plus the detail screen of a person). Every user has the same four tabs (Beranda, Resep, Cari, Profil) and the same functions. Supersedes D-007 and D-016. Write access follows **ownership**: someone else's recipe or trial opens read-only (this folds S15 and S16 into S6 and S8 as an "other" state). What each role can *see* is set by D-025. - **D-024** `2026-10-01` Layout rhythm (Alex asked for tidier margins and padding): page gutter 20; 16 between blocks; 24 between sections; 12 between list cards; card padding 16, feature cards 20; all four tab roots share one large title row; the bottom safe area is 30 for sheets and action bars; tab-bar icon and back-button glyphs line up with the gutter. Checked automatically: every block sits on the 20 px gutter on both sides, at 390 and 360 px, at text scale 1 and 1.3.
- ~~**D-025**~~ *Superseded by D-026 (no cross-user data at all).* `2026-10-01` **Visibility follows the role; the menus do not** (Alex: "students may not see other students' recipes"). A Student only ever sees their own recipes and trials. A Chef sees every student's, read-only. This keeps PRD BR-03 and CHF-01 to CHF-04 as written. In the UI it means the Cari tab exists for everyone, but for a Student it finds only their own recipes (scope Resep; the Pengguna scope says "Hanya kamu yang terlihat"), and any direct route to someone else's recipe, trial or profile shows "Tidak ada akses". The backend must enforce this (BR-03); the app only reflects it. Open: Cari is thin for a Student (it duplicates the Resep search); say if you would rather hide the tab for Students, at the cost of menus no longer being identical.
- **D-026** `2026-10-01` **No Cari tab and no Chef-specific functionality** (Alex). The Chef "sees" a student through the PDF the student sends (PDF-01), not through the app. So: three tabs for everyone (Beranda, Resep, Profil); every account only ever opens its own data; the role is a read-only badge in the profile. Removed from the prototype and the design: S13 (search), S14 (person detail), S15 and S16 (read-only views), S17 (recipe search), the visibility rules, and "Tidak ada akses". Supersedes D-017 and D-025, and the Cari parts of D-023 and D-024. **The requirement documents were changed to match:** PRD v0.4 and User Stories v0.4 (the v0.3 files are in `../_archive/`). PRD v0.4 retires CHF-01 to CHF-04, rewrites BR-02 and BR-03, and records the change as **CR-03**. Open: CR-03 touches Overview v2 (§4J, §4K, scope items #14 and #15, Phase 5) and so the price and timeline in Overview §14 and Invoice v2 Annex A, which are not edited here. RAGI must approve CR-03 in writing (Overview §13).
- **D-027** `2026-10-01` **Recipes come with a food photo and base ingredients from the admin backend** (Alex). (1) Every recipe row (Resep tab, Beranda) shows a food photo; Recipe Detail opens with a large photo. (2) Recipe Detail shows a read-only **Bahan resep** section: the recipe's base ingredients and portions, set by the admin ("Dari admin"). (3) **Trial baru** asks where to start: the recipe's base ingredients, a previous trial of the same recipe (the student's own quantities), or empty; the editor can also pull the quantities of a previous trial onto the current rows by name. A trial made from the base ingredients is labelled "Dari bahan resep". A recipe the student made has no photo (category icon on gold tint) and no base ingredients. Photos in the prototype are hotlinked from Wikimedia Commons (CC0, public domain, CC BY), credits in `03_Screens/working/photo-credits.html`; the real photos come from the admin backend. Now in the requirement documents: see D-028.
- **D-028** `2026-10-01` **Students cannot create recipes; the requirement documents were updated** (Alex). Recipes come only from the admin backend and are the same for every student. Removed from the prototype: S5 (create / edit recipe), the "+" on Recipes, the recipe menu (edit, delete), the delete-recipe dialog (O7), the duplicate-name warning (O9) and the category picker sheet (O3 is now the chip filter). "Resep" lists the whole catalogue (tried recipes first); the Dashboard lists the recipes the student has trials of ("Terakhir dicoba"); trials, trial numbers and the Stable Trial are per student. **PRD v0.5 and User Stories v0.5 are written** (v0.4 files in `../_archive/`): REC-01, REC-03, REC-04 retired; REC-02 and REC-05 reworked; TRL-05 and CPY-02 added; BR-11 and BR-12, dependency D7 and questions Q19 to Q21 added; recorded as **CR-04**, which reworks Overview §4B and needs RAGI's written approval (price and timeline unassessed). Overview v2, Invoice v2 and the Walkthrough decks are not edited.
- **D-029** `2026-10-01` **Trial Detail (S8) and Compare (S19) polish**, after the Impeccable critique of 2026-10-01 (27/40; archive in `../.impeccable/critique/`). Alex chose S8 and S19 only.
